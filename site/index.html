Did the money move?

A fund pays 6 investors through a bank that times out, loses notifications and sometimes lies. A timeout means we do not know whether the money moved, so we ask the bank before we resend. Pick a story and press Play.

The same checker grades these runs and 2,000 seeded scenarios in CI, plus five deliberately planted bugs it must catch: exit 1 means caught, and a crash is not a catch. Design and trade-offs · Source

What this does not do (yet)
  • No maker-checker approval, identity hold or cancellation: designed, not built.
  • No returns or reversals: money can only go out today.
  • MANUAL_REVIEW has no admin write path: a human resolves it outside the system.
  • USD only, integer cents, no per-currency minor units.
  • A fake bank, not a real one: no ISO 20022, no settlement cut-off times.
  • One process and a SQLite file, not a distributed deployment.

The reasoning behind those gaps is in agreed decisions.
